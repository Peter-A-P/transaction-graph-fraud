Fraud Known by the Company It Keeps

A fraud model that scores the account alone misses the fraud that lives in the links between accounts. This page shows how much, measured: three ways of scoring the same accounts in the same time-ordered split, with 95% intervals.

R scores the transaction's own row. G adds features computed from the account's neighbourhood. N is a graph neural network. TabFM is a tabular foundation model that is handed the data and not trained. Each dot is the arm's headline run, and its line is the interval over time steps or hours, never over single accounts.

Does the graph help?

Precision-recall AUC on the test period. IBM's synthetic sets leave out the generator's last few days, which are nearly all laundering.
